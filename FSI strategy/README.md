# EAI&D — FSI strategy

Working folder for the financial services strategy of the **EAI&D**
portfolio (Engineering, AI and Data).

## Start here

**[HANDOVER.md](HANDOVER.md)** — the context that is not on the slides:
drafting rules, fixed vocabulary, settled scope, decisions taken, questions
still open, and build/QA steps. Read it before editing anything.

## Files

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `EAI&D-FSI-Strategy-Development-Plan.pptx` | The deliverable. The plan for *developing* the strategy — framework only, no answers |
| `make_deck.js` | Generator. `node make_deck.js` (needs `npm install pptxgenjs`). Edit this, not the .pptx |
| `HANDOVER.md` | Full working context |

Every slide carries speaker notes. Detail, rationale and reconciliation
rules live there; the slides stay light because they are placeholders.

## Deck structure

Six parts, each opening with a divider, then an appendix of templates.

**Part one — the frame**
- How this pack runs
- What this document is — method, not conclusion
- How this fits — between the EAI&D portfolio strategy and the FSI industry strategy
- Who is doing this — the strategy development team

**Part two — the destination**
- What the vision has to earn — six drivers, agreed before the words
- Three candidate statements — lead: "Complexity, compressed"
- Principles we hold going in

**Part three — what the strategy must cover**
- The architecture — ambition, four pillars, the discipline
- The industry we mean — banking, funds, insurance, in priority order
- Tier one and tier two — where the energy goes
- Integrated by default — propositions across offerings
- Delivery and pricing economics — construct, ADR, margin
- Team, skills and enablement — capacity, capability, certification
- Why us and not them — differentiation
- Alliances — broad technology and specialised vendors
- Eminence and positioning — external and internal

**Part four — how we build it**
- How we will get there — the six-step approach
- Divide, then converge
- Five workstreams, one per pillar
- The three-week journey
- Who meets, and when
- What could derail this
- Initiatives that outlive the cycle

**Part five — after sign-off**
- Governing the execution — rhythm, scorecard, decision rights

**Part six — strategy inputs**
- Industry Solutions today · AI and Data today · Engineering today — current propositions, one table per offering
- The priority account list — the FSI industry list as it stands
- This year's numbers — current FY FSI targets and the EAI&D share

## Appendix — the strategy template

Part three shows the *shape* of the strategy; the appendix holds the strategy
itself in template form, so the completed appendix **is** the strategy record
rather than a separate write-up. Ordered by pillar, and every level-2 topic
ends in exactly one artefact.

- The strategy on a page
- Proposition one-pager
- Market prioritisation · target accounts · account revenue plan · coverage and ownership
- Proposition catalogue (an index; detail on the one-pager)
- Market pricing · our economics · three-year growth · revenue targets
- Team and talent · capability and enablement plan
- Alliance map · eminence calendar · differentiation and proof
- Operating rhythm · initiative register · execution scorecard

Each template carries notes on how to fill it and the reconciliation rules
that apply.
