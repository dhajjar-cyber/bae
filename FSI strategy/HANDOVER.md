# Handover — EAI&D FSI strategy development plan

Read this before touching the deck. It carries the context that is *not*
on the slides: the drafting rules, the decisions already taken, and the
questions still open.

## What this is

`EAI&D-FSI-Strategy-Development-Plan.pptx` is the plan for **developing**
the financial services strategy for the EAI&D portfolio. It is a framework,
not a strategy. It contains no answers — no named markets, accounts,
propositions, targets or owners. Every one of those is a placeholder
("to be defined") for the working team to fill during the three-week cycle.

Audience: the portfolio's senior leaders. Smart, time-poor, allergic to
being explained things they already know.

Generated by `node make_deck.js` (needs `npm install pptxgenjs`). Always
regenerate from the script rather than editing the .pptx, or the two drift.

## Drafting rules — do not break these

These came from the deck's owner directly. Each one was a correction.

1. **Framework only.** No answers anywhere. If a slide would state a
   conclusion, it becomes a question plus a placeholder instead.
2. **Never the word "risk".** The idea we carry is that we absorb the
   complexity clients cannot afford to get wrong. Saying "we take the risk"
   creates a legal exposure. Say complexity, not risk.
3. **No condescension, no aphorisms.** No "lessons learned" footers, no
   maxims, no explaining strategy to strategists. Four such footers were
   removed once already; do not reintroduce the style.
4. **Titles must earn their place.** "End to end", "Overview", "Next steps"
   are dead. Titles in the pack are deliberately pointed — "Where others
   stall", "AI in the engine, not on the badge", "All three, or it does not
   count".
5. **"Sooner" is banned** as a word for speed; use acceleration, compression,
   time to market.
6. **Vocabulary is fixed** (see below). Renames have happened; check for
   stale usage after any edit.
7. **Additive, not substitutive.** New themes must not displace earlier ones.
   The six vision drivers exist precisely to stop this.
8. **One document, for the working team.** No leadership vs. team split.
9. **Speaker notes on every slide.** Detail, rationale and context go in the
   notes; the slide stays light. This is non-negotiable for new slides.
10. **No parallel machinery.** One firm account plan, existing forums,
    existing eminence programme. The strategy plugs into them.

## Scope, settled

- **Industry:** FSI, three sectors in priority order — **banking**, then
  **funds** (sovereign wealth, national and development funds, fund and
  investment management), then **insurance**.
- **Markets:** Saudi, UAE, Qatar primary. Kuwait, Oman, Jordan secondary.
- **Offerings:** Industry Solutions (core-led transformation, core platforms,
  full SI) · AI and Data (enterprise and core AI, use-case activation, data
  platforms) · Engineering (cloud, integration, microservices, API,
  bespoke build).
- **Alliances:** broad technology — Google, AWS, Microsoft, Oracle.
  Specialised vendors — Temenos, Intellect, Informatica and equivalents.
  Known gap: thin coverage in funds and insurance.
- **Alignment:** this strategy sits at the intersection of the EAI&D
  portfolio strategy and the firm's FSI industry strategy.

## Principles carried in the deck

Integrated not siloed · named accounts not averages · sized and priced ·
covered by a partner · deliverable by design · **domain-specific not
agnostic** (banking is specialised and clients expect it) · **AI and
innovation embedded** (not a separate offering — every proposition carries
an AI component by design) · **one integrated market team** (every partner
and director knows the whole catalogue, positions level one, then pulls in
the specialist) · **show don't tell** (pursuits are won with demos and
showcases; proposals assembled from reusable foundations, not written from
scratch).

## Vision

Six drivers every candidate statement must carry, agreed before the words:
end-to-end accountability · complexity handled (never "risk") ·
acceleration · both halves, market-facing and internal · AI present but not
the headline · all three sectors.

Lead candidate: **"Complexity, compressed."**

> We take on the change financial institutions cannot afford to get wrong —
> modern platforms for their customers, intelligence and better tools for
> their people — brought end to end by one accountable team, with the assets
> and partnerships that compress the path.

Two alternatives sit beside it in the deck. Not yet chosen.

## Team

Dany leads overall. Tanay leads Industry Solutions, Rachel leads AI and Data,
Clifford leads Engineering. Pillar ownership is assigned from this group at
kick-off.

Each lead is a point of contact **and a conduit**: they take emerging
thinking back into their own offering, test it with the FSI partners and
directors there, and bring that view back in. The cycle ends with
socialisation to the wider FSI community, so the strategy is recognised
rather than announced.

## Approach

Six steps: **Mobilise · Baseline · Define · Quantify · Commit · Socialise**.
Three-week sprint, weekly gates. The mechanic is **divide and conquer, then
converge** — each offering lead works their own view, and a weekly
integration session abstracts, refactors and merges. Skipping the
integration session is the named failure mode.

## Structure

Two levels throughout, and one invariant: **every level-2 topic ends in
exactly one appendix artefact.** If you add a topic, add its template.

- **Part one — the frame**: what this is, how it fits, who is doing it
- **Part two — the destination**: vision drivers, three candidates, principles
- **Part three — what the strategy must cover**: the architecture, the
  industry, tiering, propositions, delivery and economics, team, alliances,
  eminence, differentiation
- **Part four — how we build it**: approach, divide-then-converge,
  workstreams, the three-week journey, who meets when, what could derail
  this, initiatives that outlive the cycle
- **Part five — after sign-off**: governing the execution
- **Part six — strategy inputs**: current propositions per offering, the FSI
  priority account list, this year's numbers
- **Appendix — the strategy template**: a blank table per artefact, so the
  completed appendix *is* the strategy record rather than a separate write-up

## Things deliberately decided

- The **proposition catalogue is an index** (proposition · offerings involved ·
  integrated or focused · sectors served · status). Detail lives on the
  proposition one-pager, which sits beside it in the appendix. This resolved
  a real duplication.
- The two economics tables are **"market pricing"** (what the market pays)
  and **"our economics"** (construct, ADR, margin). Different questions.
- **Delivery model, pricing, ADR, onshore/nearshore/offshore construct and
  target GM are one decision**, not five. Assets, accelerators and
  AI-assisted delivery sit inside the delivery model, not beside it.
- **Three-year growth is stated year by year**, by offering and by
  proposition, with a driver column per line. Not a single end-state number.
- Flagship initiative that outlives the cycle: **proposal and orals quality**
  — reusable foundations, demo-led orals.
- Eminence covers **external and internal** — internal meaning FS partners
  and directors in other portfolios.
- A glossary slide was built and then removed; the terminology is applied
  silently instead. Do not re-add it.

past the band, footers wrapping. Several were shipped and then fixed this way.

pptxgenjs gotchas that bit already: no `#` in hex colours · `isTextBox: true`

script mixes literal `—` / `·` with `—` / `·` escapes, so string
replacements need care.
